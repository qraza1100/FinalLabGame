# Storylines: The Drowned Sanctum gameplay trailer

The person asked for the file ("create a demo video ... extremely good ... minimal length"), so the recommended storyline was built directly.

## A. "POV: You Stole It" (built, recommended)
Recommended because a stranger gets it in two seconds: you grabbed the treasure, the temple is coming for you.
- **Device:** result first, then a rewind gag. **Form:** cut on the beat, with punch-ins, freeze-frame stamps and whip cuts.
- First 2 s: the sub grabs the glowing Heart, the screen turns alarm-red, ESCAPE 01:29 appears, and the caption reads "P.O.V. YOU JUST STOLE THE HEART OF ETERNITY".
- Beats: hook (stolen Heart, alarm) → "temple wants it back" → record scratch + VHS rewind → type your name, dive (menu → crane down) → a montage with one rule per beat (air, sonar, pearls, sigils + gates, currents + urchins, vents, the "or don't" game-over gag, shark) → three sigils, one Heart (cinematic reveal) → grab it, 90 s, GO (escape at speed) → escaped + score → leaderboard → end card.
- Look: real gameplay footage full-frame; captions in Archivo Black caps with a hard amber/cyan highlight box; stamps like a sticker printer.
- Sound: funky house at 128 BPM, starting on its strongest bar; record scratch and tape rewind on the gag; real game SFX on every pickup and hit; one bass impact on the title.

## B. "Any% Speedrun" (not built)
One continuous speedrun with a live split timer down the side. Each feature is a split ("Moon Sigil 0:04.2 ✓"), with speedrunner-commentary voiceover, ending on a PB (personal best) flash and the leaderboard.

## C. "Rules of the Deep" (not built)
A meme-format rule book: "Rule #1: don't run out of air." Each rule is a sticky-note card slapped over the footage that breaks it, ending with "Rule #7: get out."
