Game Description

A 3D top-down maze escape. Pilot a tiny explorer sub through a sunken temple, collect 3 sigil stones, claim the Heart of Eternity and escape before the Sanctum seals forever.

Gameplay Features
  • Move: WASD / Arrow Keys
  • Boost: Shift (burns more oxygen)
  • Oxygen drains, so refill at air vents
  • Collect 3 sigils to open matching gates
  • Collect pearls to raise your score
  • Take the Heart: 90 s escape timer
  • Win: reach the exit with the Heart
  • Lose: oxygen hits 0 or time runs out
