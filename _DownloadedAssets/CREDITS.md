Made in Blender for this project (original, script in `Blender/build_sanctum_props.py`): explorer sub, Heart of Eternity, sigil tablet, pearl, sea urchin, air vent, seaweed, coral, altar.

Used in the game build: Kenney Mini Dungeon (column, rocks, stones, pot, barrel), Quaternius Shark / Fish1-3 / Manta ray, the three ambientCG textures, the Kenney sound packs, both music tracks and both fonts. The Kenney Modular Dungeon Kit and UI Pack were downloaded as options but are not used in the final game, because the maze walls and UI are generated in code.

Original raw downloads are kept in `_zips/`.
